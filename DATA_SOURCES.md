- David Ifeoluwa Adelani and colleagues, *SIB-200*. The item IDs and topic labels derive from this release. Author files are under `data/<language>` in the pinned dataset. - MTEB contributors, SIB-200 configuration and cache source. The original per-language Parquet shards contain text and label. The locked roster selected the first exact author-text occurrence after checking label agreement across repetitions. [Pinned MTEB data](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mteb/sib200/tree/83d0320451aff3cf268beb963bf6b5f2f0bcbc3a) · [MTEB](https://aclanthology.org/2023.eacl-main.148/)
- NLLB Team and FLORES contributors, FLORES-200, mirrored by DGME. At the pinned mirror, concatenate `dev/<language>.dev` and `devtest/<language>.devtest` in that order (997 then 1,012 lines) and use the zero-based canonical index. Compare normalized text (Unicode NFC and right trim). Resolve the corresponding URL from metadata_dev.tsv and metadata_devtest.tsv. The grouping also links normalized-identical texts. The provided opaque components preserve that grouping without exposing source text or document URLs. [Pinned mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DGME/FLORES-200/tree/8a8758a392e429432f6e95fcac1445550a4d3ccd) · [FLORES-200 resource paper](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-024-07335-x)
- Sentence Transformers, paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2. The controlled heads used cached normalized 384-dimensional embeddings, maximum input length 128, without a prefix. The capsule carries the downstream scores. [Pinned model](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2/tree/e8f8c211226b894fcb81acc59f3b34ba3efd5f42)

## TextDetox

Figures 1 and 2 report the manuscript authors' aggregate measurements using the TextDetox team's *Multilingual Toxicity Detection Dataset*. The revision used was `01907546324b0330d2d8b7669648cc18823323e5`. The card identifies the compiled dataset as OpenRAIL++ and credits the original corpus authors. For the compilation and its context, see the [pinned dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/textdetox/multilingual_toxicity_dataset/blob/01907546324b0330d2d8b7669648cc18823323e5/README.md), [PAN TextDetox 2024](https://pan.webis.de/clef24/pan24-web/text-detoxification.html), [PAN TextDetox 2025](https://pan.webis.de/clef25/pan25-web/text-detoxification.html), [Dementieva et al. on Ukrainian toxicity classification](https://aclanthology.org/2024.woah-1.19/) and [Bevendorff et al. on PAN 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-56072-9_1).
